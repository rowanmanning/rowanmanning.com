    This week my youngest brother, George, turned 30. I took the day off to go home and see him, I think the last time we were _all_ together at home was more than 7 years ago which is ridiculous but understandable now that we all do separate Christmas etc and often meet somewhere in the middle of all our places.

    It was such a lovely day. We reminisced, we cooked together, and we gently bullied mum as a team for the first time in forever.

    Mum was ecstatic that we all agreed to replicate a family photo from nearly 20 years ago. Look at all of us little babies. George and I nailed the pose.

  * {{< details "Content warning: sickness" >}}
      George has been pretty sick with the chemo this time around. He started the initial rounds feeling pretty invinsible but the new treatment and steroids mean he has a pretty bad time of it. Cancer talk didn't dominate the day but obviously it's there, he's looking pretty good generally and has been building muscle back up; his appetite has returned. What a weird thing eh?
    {{< /details >}}

  * On Thursday we went to a vegan pizza themed pub quiz at Purezza with a few friends. The quiz _was_ quite pizza-focused but we managed joint third place. It was a nice time and the pizza was pretty good; excellent sauce portions. One too many wines maybe.

  * On Friday night we drove up to Matlock with the lads, we booked the week off and it'll be nice to relax a bit more. We're assuming it'll be easier to get some house tasks done when we're not also trying to work.

  * Saturday was spent doing a _lot_ of jobs. We steam-cleaned the kitchen floor for four hours and got the house ready for more frequent habitation. Charlotte's family arrived the next day and we put together a pretty excellent BBQ. We'll be heading out on some countryside walks and just having a chill time for the next few days. Once they leave the work will continue!

  * I finished [the first Mistborn book](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Mistborn:_The_Final_Empire) really quickly and have picked up [the second](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Mistborn:_The_Well_of_Ascension). Very good, can't put them down. Something that is good is that I'm no longer chasing the reading streak so there's no pressure to read every single day if I don't find time. This will hopefully keep me sustained on this reading peak for longer instead of feeling despondent when I miss a day and giving up entirely.
