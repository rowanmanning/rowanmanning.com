  * We started this week going to see Abigail's Party at the Harold Pinter Theatre, it was great! Only realised afterwards that it starred Lauren Patel who was great in [Small Prophets](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Small_Prophets). There was some weird nostalgia for me – we watched and ran through scenes for it in GCSE drama and I kept thinking of staging notes from my very eccentric drama teacher.

  * On Tuesday we ran our first (pilot) Unite social at the FT and had lunch on the roof with members who were in the office. It had a nice level of attendance and we'll be running more in future!

  * A few too many wines after work but gotta make the most of the tail end of summer.

  * On Wednesday I wandered over to Wapping to meet Karol for lunch. We sat by the river and caught up a bunch, nice to have another nearby friend! I haven't spoken to him in about a decade so has been funny seeing him twice in as many weeks.

  * The weekend was dominated by [Camp Wildfire](https://campwildfire.co.uk/), a name I'm sure they regret slightly with recent news. Their fire regulations were really strict before the rain began.

  * Our tent leaked quite a bit initially and several poles broke. I have some fiberglass cuts and splinters to show for it. _Despite_ the adversity I had a nice weekend, but maybe I'm a fairweather camper.

  * {{< pin right >}}{{< image "blacksmithing.jpg" >}}{{< /pin >}}

    This year I did blacksmithing, air rifle shooting, pyrography, and a botanical walk. Fewer but more expensive activities than [last year](/weeknotes/252/). The blacksmithing was a lot of fun, I made a little keyring to learn a bunch of techniques and I'd like to do some more.

  * We ignored most of the patrol stuff this year as I find it a bit intense but the whole thing was good fun. My new wellies look a lot less new.
