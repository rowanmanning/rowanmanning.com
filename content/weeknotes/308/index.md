    We have the week booked off as holiday which _mostly_ means intense scrubbing. This week we focused on the grout in the showers, it involved some heavy cleaning and then painting the grout white again. It looks a lot better and is quite satisfying tbh.

  * I played online board games in Matlock for the first time, new setup is great, I did badly at the game though. It's quite nice being able to play without headphones because I'm so far away from the other house occupants.

  * On Wednesday I took Charlotte to Stones for a tasting menu as a late birthday present. It was really great and included a wine pairing with each course :thumbs_up: the menu was a little more Rowan-coded than Charlotte-coded because they changed it a week before we came, so well done for me.

  * On Thursday we drove up to Huddersfield to collect a bed frame from Leigh-Ann & Andy, met their new kid, and ate a katsu which was excellent. The bed _barely_ fit in the car and I had to drive back without easy access to the handbrake. It was fine. Now our guests can have more than a mattress on the floor!

  * Speaking of guests, Luke arrived later in the day and got to be the first to try out the new bed. The next day we got a bus to a nearby farm shop and cafe then walked the 5 miles back. It was delightful, lots of varied landscapes, and some excellent mushroom and stilton toast.

  * Gallal arrived later. We showed him Matlock and then we all went on a longer walk near Elton which included [Robin Hood's Stride](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Robin_Hood%27s_Stride). We ate our sandwiches amongst the rocks.

  * This post tips my "Weeknote Words" stat to over 100,000. That's a hundred thousand words of me rambling and it _should_ take an average reader a solid 11 hours to get through them. That's more words than The Hobbit and I fully believe my collective works will have as much of a cultural impact.

  * I've done absolutely masses of D&D world-building this week. I wrote a first edition of my world's newspaper, fleshed out the economy, and finished a lot of admin tasks like adding wiki entries for 29 different forests, 7 seas, 3 lakes, 36 rivers, and 20 bays and gulfs.
