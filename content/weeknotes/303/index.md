  * We spent a lot of the week with Charlotte's family in Matlock, starting with a BBQ after they arrived (and just before the nationwide alert :grimacing:) we had a lovely time and I think they enjoyed staying with us. We had booked the week off and it was a great idea, not trying to squeeze any work in.

  * We walked over to Matlock Bath one of the days, hired pedal-boats on the river, and ate chips. The heat was a bit much really but the walk was nice anyway.

  * On Tuesday we came second in the local pub quiz _again_ with the help of Charlotte's brother and his girlfriend. It's a fun quiz and we'll probs go every time we're up.
